OpenSim run with the knee actuator: patient two months after ACL reconstruction

This run adds the final bladder actuator (k = 206 990 mm³) to the gait2392 model. It covers the two assist windows, the two bladder pressure limits and six values of α, and its results are compared with the values calculated in the final report and with the clinical constraints.

Patient: man 70 kg · 1.70 m · 2 months after ACLR Stance time: 0.705 s Conditions: 44 Software: OpenSim 4.6 · Static Optimization

Interpretation: the knee and the muscles during assistance

  • Required muscle moment (H1): at the peak (21 %) it falls from 8.38 to 4.24 N·m with the 20 kPa limit (51 % remains on the muscles) and to 2.81 N·m with the 27 kPa limit at α = 0.169 (34 % remains). With an ideal device at α = 0.20, 21 % remains, the same value as in the H1 calculation of the biomechanics data.
  • Restoration (H2): if the patient keeps working and the device adds to the patient's moment, the knee moment reaches 30.9 % of healthy with the 20 kPa limit and 34.2 % with the 27 kPa limit, against 20.9 % without the device. The target of 36.6 % at α = 0.20 is not reached with either limit.
  • Window option 1 and the knee: at 8.43–13 % and 36–44 % the patient's own moment is flexor (negative) while the device adds an extension moment, so the muscles must supply a larger flexor moment. The result is hamstrings +20 N (α 0.125) to +33 N (α 0.20) and gastrocnemius +15 to +24 N, i.e. co-contraction and a higher load on the knee joint. The hamstrings pull the tibia backwards and unload the ACL graft, but a higher joint load is undesirable in the second month.
  • Window option 2 and the knee: in no frame does the device exceed the patient's own moment, and the summed knee muscle force falls. In the frames limited by the cap (4 of 24 at α 0.125) the muscle moment becomes almost zero, i.e. the muscles do no work there if the patient relaxes.
  • Device work: the negative work is larger than the positive work (e.g. −0.22 / +0.08 J per step, window option 1, α 0.125). The device resists knee flexion in early stance (9–21 %) more than it assists extension; this matches the stiff-knee pattern at two months and requires clinical assessment.
  • α = 0.50: without a pressure limit it needs 79.6 kPa in window option 1 and exceeds the patient's own moment in all 36 frames. With a 20 or 27 kPa limit it gives almost the same result as α = 0.20.
! Limitation
All muscle results here assume that the Model B torque (p × k) reaches the knee in full and that the knee motion does not change with the device (H1). The full-device FEA in the report (Section 6.3) predicts that only 0.08–0.12 N·m reaches the knee; if that value applies, the quadriceps reduction would be only about 1 %. The model also contains no ACL, so it gives no graft strain, and the device mass (374 g) is not included.
